## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-precision, utilitarian, and dependable digital operating environment for shared agricultural assets and fleet management. It bridges heavy-duty field reliability with cutting-edge SaaS logistical software.

### Tone & Personality
- **Utilitarian & Grounded:** Clean lines, structured telemetry tables, and high-visibility status cues, avoiding decorative excess in favor of clear operational data.
- **Trustworthy & Robust:** High visual contrast, solid foundations, and calm natural tones inspire confidence for high-value machinery rentals and operations.
- **Field-Ready Modernity:** Modern software clarity tailored for varied lighting conditions, from high-glare field environments to low-light depot offices.

### Visual Movement
The interface relies on **Modern Functionalist / Contemporary Agritech**:
- Warm neutral canvas backdrops replacing sterile cool greys with organic, soil-inspired undertones.
- Heavy structural framing with subtle tactile dividers.
- Dense information hierarchy built around operational states, GPS tracking stats, and telematics.

## Colors

The palette grounds high-tech fleet optimization into an agricultural setting by pairing deep pine canopy depths with energetic vegetative greens and fertile earthen hues.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#1E3A2B`):** Deep Forest Green. Serves as the primary structural color for headers, dominant interactive buttons, key navigation elements, and brand presence.
- **Secondary (`#10B981`):** Vivid Leaf Green. Applied selectively for interactive highlights, key metric signals, successful states, and real-time operational availability badges.
- **Tertiary (`#8D7B68`):** Muted Loam Brown. Deployed for secondary technical specifications, machine hours, engine ratings, equipment classification tags, and auxiliary metadata.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F4F3EE`):** Warm Sand-White. Serves as the underlying container background, reducing screen glare while introducing organic warmth.

### Semantic Tones
- **Operational Available (`#10B981` on `#ECFDF5`):** Signal for unreserved, deployable equipment.
- **Reserved / Scheduled (`#D97706` on `#FFFBEB`):** Active booking or upcoming field deployment.
- **Maintenance / Down (`#EF4444` on `#FEF2F2`):** Mechanical fault, calibration, or required service interval.
- **In Transit (`#0284C7` on `#F0F9FF`):** Asset currently moving between regional depot hubs.

### Contrast Standards
All interactive text and functional graphics conform to strict WCAG AAA guidelines against both the warm sand canvas and dark forest green components.

## Typography

The design system standardizes on **Plus Jakarta Sans** for comprehensive typographic hierarchy, paired with **JetBrains Mono** for serial numbers, VINs, and real-time telemetric sensor readings.

### Rationale & Characteristics
- **Readability & Geometry:** Plus Jakarta Sans delivers structural legibility with crisp geometric terminals and modern proportions, remaining clear even on rugged, low-resolution mobile displays.
- **Tabular Data:** JetBrains Mono is assigned to machine telemetry, GPS coordinates, implement hookup configurations, and pricing tiers to ensure aligned data columns.
- **Hierarchy Scaling:** Larger sizes compress letter spacing slightly to ensure titles remain compact, while sub-12px badges enforce uppercase styling with widened tracking (`0.05em`) for quick glanceability.

## Layout & Spacing

A modular 12-column responsive fluid grid underpins the equipment directory, booking calendars, and dashboard layouts.

### Screen Adaptations
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, 16px outer margin (`margin-sm`), 12px gutter (`gutter-sm`). Actions dock to bottom sheets for one-handed operation in the field.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 8-column layout, 24px outer margin, 16px gutter. Machine cards reflow into two-column listings.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout, 32px outer margin (`margin`), 24px gutter (`gutter`). Split-pane layout with continuous map/telemetry tracking alongside asset catalogues.

### Rhythm Principles
- Component internals follow consistent multi-step spacing: 8px (`space-sm`) between tags and titles, 16px (`space-md`) between structural content blocks, and 24px (`space-lg`) card interior padding for primary dashboard elements.

## Elevation & Depth

To sustain an architectural, clean, and utilitarian dashboard feel, the design system avoids heavy drop shadows and blurry light fields. Instead, it utilizes **tonal elevation layers** framed with **low-contrast earthen outlines**.

### Surface Levels
- **Canvas Base (`#F4F3EE`):** The default screen layer.
- **Card / Panel Surface (`#FBFBF9`):** Pure light surface sitting immediately above the base canvas.
- **Elevated Interactive Layer (`#FFFFFF`):** Flyout drawers, dropdown menus, and popovers.

### Border and Stroke Hierarchy
- **Standard Containment Border:** `1px solid #EFECE6`. Applied across cards, table rows, and toolbar sections to define borders cleanly under direct sun.
- **Active / Focused Border:** `1px solid #1E3A2B` or `2px solid #10B981` for active machinery selections.

### Ambient Shadows
- **Base Components:** Flat with border bounds (`box-shadow: none`).
- **Floating Overlays & Popovers:** `box-shadow: 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(30, 58, 43, 0.08), 0 1px 3px rgba(30, 58, 43, 0.04)`. A targeted deep-forest-tinted shadow prevents floating elements from appearing disconnected while maintaining softness.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates **Soft (Level 1)** geometric curves. It maintains an engineered, machine-tooled aesthetic that feels purposeful rather than bubbly or purely consumer-oriented.

### Radius Hierarchy
- **Micro Radius (4px / `0.25rem`):** Badges, status tags, form inputs, checkboxes, and inline code blocks.
- **Standard Component Radius (8px / `0.5rem`):** Operational buttons, equipment summary cards, modal dialogues, and alert banners.
- **Container Radius (12px / `0.75rem`):** Large map overlay panels, fleet overview containers, and master data tables.
- **Pill (9999px):** Restricted strictly to circular icon buttons, telemetry live-pulsers, and toggle switches.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#1E3A2B`, text `#FFFFFF`, hover `#14281E`. 8px border-radius, font weight 600. High-contrast, deliberate, and sturdy.
- **Secondary:** Background `#F4F3EE`, border `1px solid #8D7B68`, text `#1E3A2B`, hover `#EFECE6`.
- **Accent (Action/Deploy):** Background `#10B981`, text `#FFFFFF`, hover `#059669`. Used strictly for checkout, rental confirmation, and live asset activation.
- **Focus Ring:** Explicit double ring: `outline: 2px solid #10B981; outline-offset: 2px`.

### Badges & Status Chips
- **Structural Spec Badges:** Background `#EFECE6`, text `#8D7B68`, font weight 600, uppercase letter-spacing `0.05em`. Used for horsepower, attachment types, and drive configurations.
- **Availability State Indicators:**
  - *Available:* Dot `#10B981`, surface `#ECFDF5`, border `#A7F3D0`, text `#065F46`.
  - *Reserved:* Dot `#D97706`, surface `#FFFBEB`, border `#FDE68A`, text `#92400E`.
  - *Maintenance:* Dot `#EF4444`, surface `#FEF2F2`, border `#FECACA`, text `#991B1B`.
  - *In Transit:* Dot `#0284C7`, surface `#F0F9FF`, border `#BAE6FD`, text `#075985`.

### Form Controls
- **Inputs & Dropdowns:** Base surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #E6DFD5`, text `#1F2937`. Height 44px for field-touch accessibility. Placeholder `#8D7B68`.
- **Active State:** Border `#1E3A2B`.
- **Validation Error:** Border `#EF4444`, focus ring `2px solid #EF4444` with inline micro-copy in `#991B1B`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 20px dimension, border `#8D7B68`, checked background `#1E3A2B` with crisp white mark.

### Utilitarian Equipment Cards
- Surface `#FBFBF9`, border `1px solid #EFECE6`, internal padding `16px`.
- **Header:** Asset title, model, and category accompanied by live status badge right-aligned.
- **Spec Grid:** 2-column or 3-column micro-matrix detailing HP, fuel level/battery, current depot hub location, and operator certification requirements.
- **Footer:** Pricing per engine hour / day in dark forest green, with secondary action ("Inspect") and primary action ("Book / Dispatch").

### Telemetry & Fleet Tracking Strips
- Compact inline components for machinery tracking: live mini-barometer for fuel level, engine hours ticker using `code-sm` font, and breadcrumb indicator showing the route between regional hubs.
